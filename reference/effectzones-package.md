# effectzones: Interpretive Background Zones for Estimates and Confidence Intervals

A 'ggplot2' extension that draws interpretive background zones behind
estimates and confidence intervals in forest plots, post-hoc contrast
plots and coefficient plots. Two modes are provided: effect-size
magnitude zones based on published benchmark sets (e.g. Cohen, 1988,
[doi:10.4324/9780203771587](https://doi.org/10.4324/9780203771587) ) and
clinical relevance zones based on a minimal clinically important
difference (Jaeschke, Singer and Guyatt, 1989
[doi:10.1016/0197-2456(89)90005-6](https://doi.org/10.1016/0197-2456%2889%2990005-6)
). The package does not compute, classify or reinterpret inference; it
only draws a background against which existing estimates and intervals
are read.
